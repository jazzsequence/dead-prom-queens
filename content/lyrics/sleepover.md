### Verse 1
We lay out our sleeping bags in the den
Her mom says lights out at eleven
Her mom says the house is old, it makes sounds
Her mom says don’t mind the sounds

### Verse 2
Jenny breathes slow and funny on purpose
She has decided to be asleep
I close my eyes the way she does
We are very good at it

### Verse 3
The hallway light comes on under the door
It clicks the same way every time
The third board creaks, the weight of it
She saved me the spot nearest the hall

### Verse 4

He sits down on the edge of my bag
He says shh, he says I’m only checking
He says I’m his favorite
I look at the water stain on the ceiling
It looks like a rabbit if you let it
Jenny’s back is to me
Her breathing hasn’t changed

Jenny says great
And I say great
